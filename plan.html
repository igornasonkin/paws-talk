Plan the next episodes. Change a topic, its focus, the length, the voices and the date. Then press Submit request. It opens Messages with everything written out. Nothing is sent until you press Send.

The Show Planner is for the show’s owner. Turn it on for this phone only.

Where should requests go?

Saved on this phone only. It is never put on the website.

Weekly rhythm

A new episode is made every day you tick. Unless you choose one, Claude picks a calm topic at random on the day.

    What happens after you send

    1. Submit request opens Messages with the plan written out, addressed to your number. Press Send. That is your record.
    2. Send to Claude opens the share sheet. Tap the Claude app, then send. Claude cannot read iPhone texts, so this is how the daily episode maker gets your change.
    3. Every morning Claude makes that day’s episode with the AI voices. The Legal Desk checks it. If it is GREEN, it goes live in this app by itself. If not, it waits for your “publish”.
    4. Dates you did not change get a calm surprise topic. Journal Club gets a real dog science paper from the news or the journals.
