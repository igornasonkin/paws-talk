How a first stay works

  1. Text us your dates and your dog’s name and age.
  2. Meet and greet. We meet you and your dog before the first stay.
  3. Share vaccination records from your veterinarian.
  4. Confirm together. We agree on the dates and the quote, then the stay is booked.

Which vaccinations are required?

All guests must be up to date on rabies, DHPP (distemper, hepatitis, parvovirus, and parainfluenza), and Bordetella (kennel cough). Please share your veterinarian’s records before the first stay.
