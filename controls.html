    Where do reminders and new-episode messages appear?

    They come as notifications from the Claude app, not inside Walk & Talk. If you never see them:

    1. Open your iPhone Settings.
    2. Scroll down and tap Apps (on older iPhones skip this step), then tap Claude.
    3. Tap Notifications and turn on Allow Notifications.
    4. Tap a notification to read the full message in the Claude app.
